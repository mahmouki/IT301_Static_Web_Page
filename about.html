Why Walk Harrisonburg?

What originally was just a way for me to de-stress, turned into an activity I like to do often! Sometimes I walk with a destination in mind, and sometimes I don't. I've started writing poetry on these walks as well, it can be a very nice creative outlet for me!

If you're interested in the city, check out Visitharrisonburgva.com and if you're interested in seeing more of my photos and poems, vist My Instagram page!
