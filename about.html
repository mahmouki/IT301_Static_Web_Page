About the photographer

Why I walk with a camera

Walking slows me down enough to notice details: a mural half hidden in an alley, the way light hits the courthouse square. Every photo here was taken on foot in Harrisonburg, Virginia.

What I carry

  • One camera and one lens
  • A small notebook for locations
  • Comfortable shoes
